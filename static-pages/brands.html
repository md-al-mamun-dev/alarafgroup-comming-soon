Al-Araf Fashion

Sustainable Agro-Commodities

Al-Araf Agro

Agri-Produce, Grain & Cold-Chain Logistics

Empowering regional farmers while maintaining strict international phytosanitary standards for bulk food grains, seasonal crops, and agro-commodities.

Al-Araf Agro works directly with certified farming clusters across fertile delta belts in Bangladesh, deploying modern collection hubs and temperature-controlled post-harvest logistics.

Core Capabilities & Standards
